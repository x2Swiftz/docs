### ✅ Our **Auto Sell** will sell items based on the **Rarity** you choose

<iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/GoeVJXazL4U" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowfullscreen />

**For example**, if you select **Epic**, it will sell all **Epic** items, as well as lower rarities like **Uncommon** and **Common**

For **Category**, you can choose which type of items you want to sell. The options are **Weapon, Skills, Armor, and Helmet**

For **Keep Items,** You can choose which items you don’t want to sell like Valhalla Mage Armor. You can select multiple items too, just search for the item name and tick the ones you want

The **Sell List** shows which types of items you’ve selected to sell. Once you’ve checked everything you want to sell, it’ll show up there automatically
