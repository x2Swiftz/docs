## ✅ Step 1 — Choose Your Dungeon

Create a Dungeon to farm by choosing the **Dungeon** and **Difficulty** you can handle 

If you can't finish it before time runs out, I recommend farming the previous Dungeon first to level up

<Note>
  Northern Lands not supported with Hardcore Mode
</Note>

## ⚔️ Step 2 — Auto Farm

Go to the `Auto Farm` tab and enable:

- `Auto Farm`

- `Auto Attack`
- `Auto Skills`

### 🤔 What is Attack Range

It’s basically the range for using skills. Once enemies are within that range, it’ll automatically cast the skill. And of course, it supports both Warrior and Mage classes

## 🔄 Step 3 — Auto Replay

To make sure it keeps farming, don't forget to turn on **Auto Replay** so it will repeat the Dungeon

If you're worried about your inventory getting full, you can also turn on **Auto Sell** and **Auto Back to Lobby**

For example, if you set **Back to Lobby After 5 Times**, it will farm the Dungeon 5 times first, then go back to the lobby and sell your items automatically

### 🕝 What is Auto Restart

It’s useful for people who can’t beat the Dungeon they’re farming and don’t wanna waste too much time

For example, if you can’t kill the final boss and don’t wanna sit there waiting for the whole run to end, you can set a timer to **Auto Restart** and start the run again automatically

## ⚡ Step 4 — Recommended Settings

For anyone who wants to let the script farm while doing other things, we recommend turning on **Whitescreen** to reduce CPU usage. It works automatically when you **minimize Roblox**.

To reduce CPU usage even more, we also recommend keeping **Low Quality** enabled. For **FPS Cap**, we recommend setting it between **30–60 FPS** for better performance and stability while Auto Farm is running.

With these settings, you can leave Auto Farm running for **24 hours** while you sleep or do other things without having to worry about it
